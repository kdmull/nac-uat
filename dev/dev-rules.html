League Format

NAC runs six leagues each season: one singles league and five doubles leagues divided by age and skill band:

  • All Ages Beginner — under 3.0 rating
  • 19–49 Intermediate and 50+ Intermediate — 3.0 to 3.4 rating
  • 19–49 Advanced and 50+ Advanced — 3.5 and up rating
  • Singles — open to all skill levels

Each league plays a round-robin season so that players or teams face a rotating set of opponents week to week.

Match Play & Scoring

Each weekly matchup is a best-of-three series — the player or team that wins two games wins the series. Enter all three game scores when a third game is played; the winner is decided by games won.

Games are played to 11 points, win by 2. (Confirm your point target — adjust here if your leagues use a different number.)

Players report their own scores from their dashboard, or scores are entered by the league organizer. Submitted results count toward DUPR for players whose DUPR accounts are linked.

Schedule & Byes

The season schedule is posted once the organizer publishes it; until then, the league page shows the roster rather than standings. Your personal weekly matchups appear on your dashboard.

When a league has an odd number of players or teams, one sits out each week on a rotating bye. A bye is not counted as a win or a loss.

DUPR & Ratings

A linked DUPR account is required before registering for a league. Linking lets your league results count toward your DUPR rating and keeps players in the correct skill band.

Your current doubles and singles ratings are shown on your profile and on your player page once your account is connected.

Standings & Tiebreakers

Standings are ordered by win percentage. When players are tied, the next tiebreaker is point differential (total points scored minus points allowed).
